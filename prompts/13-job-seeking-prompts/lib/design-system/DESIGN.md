# Juan Jaramillo #AI — Design System

## Brand Identity & Vision

Juan Jaramillo represents full-stack AI engineering, generative AI specialization, and executive technology leadership. The visual identity fuses high-tech developer iconography (CLI terminal prompt `>_`) with modern, dark-mode cyberpunk aesthetic gradients.

## Color Palette Tokens

- **Primary (Indigo)**: `#6366F1`
- **Secondary (Purple)**: `#A855F7`
- **Accent (Magenta/Pink)**: `#EC4899`
- **Dark Background**: `#09090B`
- **Surface / Card**: `#18181B`
- **Text Primary**: `#FAFAFA`
- **Text Muted**: `#A1A1AA`

## Typography

- **Primary Font**: League Spartan (Google Fonts)
- **Fallback Hierarchy**: `League Spartan`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`
- **Style Rules**: Geometric, bold headers with clean corporate legibility.

## Layout & Components

- **Logo Icon**: Hexagonal emblem containing the terminal symbol `>_` wrapped in the signature purple-to-pink gradient.
- **Glassmorphism Panels**: Dark translucent surfaces with subtle white borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`) and background blur.
- **Buttons & CTAs**: Gradient fill or sharp high-contrast border with glowing hover states.
